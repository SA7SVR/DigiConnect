Installed. What to do now
=========================

1. Open DigiConnect and enter your callsign in Settings. For DMR enter your
   DMR ID and the master password; for NXDN enter your NXDN ID.
2. Choose microphone and speakers (Settings > Audio) and press "Test microphone".
   If the level is low, raise "Microphone volume".
3. Choose a network (C4FM, D-STAR, DMR...), pick a station from the list and press Listen.

Updates
  New versions download by themselves and start at the next launch, without a new
  installation. The "Updates" window shows the version, what is new and the progress.
  When a new installer is needed (an engine change) the program tells you.

If something does not work
  The program writes what it really did into digiconnect.log
  (inside the DigiConnect data folder under %APPDATA%; type %APPDATA% in Explorer and search for "digiconnect.log"). Send us that file together with your description.

Licences
  DigiConnect contains open-source parts with their own licences; see the LICENSES
  folder inside the installation folder.
