DigiConnect 2.3.0 — HELLAS Zone (SA7SVR)
=====================================

What it is
  A program for radio amateurs that connects to digital-voice networks
  (DMR, D-STAR, C4FM/YSF, NXDN, P25, M17) to listen and to talk.
  You connect to any server in the world from the public lists.

What is new in this version (2.3.0)
  • The licence is bound to your computer and signed by the server: a copy of the program cannot be
    moved to another computer by copying the settings folder. If you change computer, the
    administrator frees the licence with one button.
  • "Licence" button in the top bar: licence status, activation code, messages from the owner with
    an unread counter, replies, and writing a message to the owner.
  • A demo cannot go on past its end, not even without a connection.
  • EchoLink: under construction - coming soon (the page is there, it does not connect yet).
  New in 2.2.0:
  • NEW: AllStarLink. Sign in with your own AllStarLink portal account (callsign +
    password), search the worldwide node list (or type a node number) and listen / talk.
    EXPERIMENTAL: it works only with nodes that accept AllStarLink app connections, and it has
    not yet been tried against the live network. The password is never saved on disk.
    This version includes a new protocol engine: install it with the full installer
    (the automatic update cannot replace the engine).
  Also new in 2.0.5:
  • C4FM: reception is now smooth. The program keeps a short reserve (about 0.3 s) of
    received speech and plays it at an exact pace, so late network packets no longer
    cut the sound. (The sound is heard about 0.3 s later.)
  • The speaker button now shows its state: a red crossed speaker = muted, an open
    speaker = sound on (also when you press F4 = mute all).
  • Bigger logo: the empty margin of the picture is cut, so it is now clearly larger.
  • Notices (e.g. "Connected ...") now appear at the top of the window, fade out after a
    few seconds and no longer cover the controls at the bottom.
  Also new in 2.0.4:
  • Microphone volume knob (0-300%) next to the PTT buttons and in the bottom bar:
    drag, mouse wheel or arrow keys; double-click = 100%. Shows the live mic level.
  • Bigger logo and the "HELLAS ZONE NET - DIGICONNECT" title back in the top bar.
  • Green outlines on cards, tabs, panels and controls, so the look is not only blue.
  Also new in 2.0.3:
  • Your station appears as "online" in the HELLAS Zone control panel (callsign,
    program version, what you listen to). You can switch this off in
    Settings > My station. No audio or passwords are ever sent.
  • New installer with our logo, Greek/English, shortcuts with the DigiConnect icon.
  • Updates window with version, what is new, size and real download progress.
  Also new since 2.0.1:
  • Microphone: the very low level on C4FM is fixed. New "Microphone volume"
    setting (0-300%) and real detection of the microphone's sample format.
  • "Refresh devices", "Test microphone", "Test speakers" work and show a result
    (signal level or a test tone).
  • Listen volume and mute now really change the sound you hear.
  • Every station shows the host and port of its server, read from its list;
    you can change them and go back to the list value.
  • Lists: DMR talkgroups by number, a country selector, and the download status of
    each list in Settings. No entry is invented - everything comes from a real list.
  • "Last heard": a structured list with UTC time, station, destination, network, duration.
  • Greek in more places in the program.

Before you continue
  If DigiConnect is open, Setup will close it. Your settings, favorites and
  history are NOT deleted.
